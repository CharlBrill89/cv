About

“Engineer by trade, problem solver by passion.”

I trained as a mechanical engineer, picked up an MBA along the way, and ended up in data engineering. That path means I tend to see problems as systems, judge work by what it changes for the business, and build with scale in mind from the start.

I've spent more than ten years in data engineering, analytics consulting, and product leadership, first in South Africa and now in Canada. The biggest lesson from that time is that a data solution only matters if people trust it enough to use it. I keep that in mind whether I'm building a medallion lakehouse on Microsoft Fabric, writing ETL pipelines in Databricks, or advising a client on data strategy. In every case the job is to take something complicated and make it clear.
